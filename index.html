Screen recorder · runs in your browser · nothing uploaded

What to record

Opens your browser's picker on that tab. You can still switch inside the picker.

Quality

Smooth suits most PCs. Use Light on older ones. Sharp (60fps) costs the most.

While recording

Floating controls keep Pause, Resume and Stop on top while you work in other windows. Live preview off saves CPU.

Audio

Windows/ChromeOS can share full system sound. On Mac, only tab sound is available.
